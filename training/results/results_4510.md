## 5. Prediction and confidence intervals

For a new company with inputs x, the app reports:

* **Prediction interval** (where that company's ROA should land): ŷ ± t × √(s² + x′Vx)
* **Confidence interval** (average ROA of companies with those ratios): ŷ ± t × √(x′Vx)
