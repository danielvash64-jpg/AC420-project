## 5. 95% range used in the app

The app reports **forecast ± 2 × s**, where s = 0.0844 is the residual standard error above (how far the model's predictions typically miss actual ROA). About 95% of companies land within two standard errors of the prediction, assuming the misses are roughly bell-shaped.

For this industry the 95% range is **forecast ± 16.9%**. (The exact t-value for 95% with 272 degrees of freedom is 1.969; 2 is the standard rounded value.)
